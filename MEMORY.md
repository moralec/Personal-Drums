# Memory: Mini Drummer

- **Audience is a 3.5-year-old** (Carlos, 2026-10-29). Real kit at home is a toy drum (head = snare, rim = hi-hat) and a red metal cup (crash). Design for big targets and no reading.
- **Songs come from MIDI**, converted at build time like the guitar pipeline (Carlos: "same as the guitar with node, and synth is fine"). Backing is a built-in synth, not samples.
- **Kick has no target**: it plays in the backing only. Open hat goes to the cup (accent), ride to the rim.
- **Easy/Medium/Busy** thinning places accents first (cup, head, rim); an earlier time-ordered pass dropped crashes behind snare hits.
- **Source files are the owner's:** Carlos owns the CDs of the songs he'll convert. A CD rip is audio, not MIDI; a MIDI has to be supplied or made from the audio.
- Headless Chrome's audio clock stays at 0 under `--virtual-time-budget`, so timing can't be tested that way; test by ear in a real browser.
- **Public repo, private songs** (Carlos, 2026-10-29): repo `moralec/Personal-Drums` is public; copyrighted songs live in `midi/private/` and `songs-private.js` (gitignored) and are deployed to Netlify only by `./deploy.sh`. Never link the Netlify site to GitHub (it would deploy without them) and never commit those files.
- **Netlify CLI takes the project id, not the name**, for `--site` (`drums-ag-insights` -> a341d2f6-…). ag-insights.co.uk DNS is at IONOS, not Netlify: a new subdomain needs a CNAME added by hand.
- **Look and feel = guitar app** (Carlos, 2026-10-29): dark `#0f0f0f`, amber `#f5a623`, Helvetica Neue, segmented controls. Toddler targets stay big.
- **Full MIDI plays, drums included; lights must match the hits exactly** (Carlos). So "Every hit" lights all mapped hits and the visuals subtract output latency. Easy/Medium are subsets of what is heard. Kick: audible, no target.
- **Art is generated, effects are measured:** Carlos generates the picture from a prompt; `tools/measure.py` finds the shapes by colour. The artwork's background is about `#131313`, not `#0f0f0f`; the stage uses `#131313` to avoid a visible seam.
- **Head glow is the middle of the drumhead only** (Carlos, 2026-10-29: "a bit narrower, in the center"): `HEAD_GLOW` in `tools/measure.py`. **Timeline** under the stage: 3 rows, playhead left to right, paged by 8 beats.
- **Levels are note values** (Carlos, 2026-10-29: "every 4th, 8th and 16th ... more logical and universal"): Quarters / Eighths / 16ths, nested, no gap or priority thinning. The first design (gap rules, accents first) was dropped. Consequence: Livin' on a Prayer at Quarters is still 2.8 hits/s (a hat on every beat), much busier than the old Easy (0.8/s).
- **Levels are note values plus a full one** (Carlos, 2026-10-29: "every 4th, 8th and 16th ... more logical and universal", then "PLUS all hits"): Quarters / Eighths / 16ths / All hits, nested, no gap or priority thinning. The first design (gap rules, accents first) was dropped. - **Five levels, named Level 1-5** (Carlos, 2026-10-29: "add halfs to make it even easier and change the names to levels"): half notes / quarters / eighths / 16ths / every hit. Livin' on a Prayer: 397 / 692 / 937 / 965 / 1006 hits (1.6 / 2.8 / 3.7 / 3.8 / 4.0 per s). A snare on beats 2 and 4 is not on the half-note grid, so Twinkle's Level 1 has no drum-head hits; Livin' has 186.

- **Rim = hi-hat only; the ride goes to the cup** (Carlos, 2026-10-29: "something that is not a high hat is being added to the rim"). - **Timeline page edges are the same instant** (Carlos, 2026-10-29): the last line of a page is the first line of the next. Both are drawn, and a hit exactly on the boundary shows on both, so it is visible one page early.

- **Installable and offline** (Carlos, 2026-10-29): `manifest.webmanifest`, icons from `tools/icon.py` (the drum from `img/source/scene.png`; needs pillow), and `sw.js` (network first, cache fallback; bump `CACHE` to drop old files). - **Real recordings play instead of the synth** (Carlos, 2026-10-30: "add the real mp3, align it with the midi"). Per song: `tools/align.py` -> `audio/<slug>.m4a` + `-slow.m4a` (pre-stretched 0.6x, pitch kept) + `audio` in `songs.json`; `build.js` remaps all song times onto the recording's clock. `audio/` is gitignored (public repo), shipped by `deploy.sh`. Synth stays for songs without a recording (Twinkle) and as fallback.
- **How a recording gets aligned (applies to every song):** `python3 tools/align.py <slug> <recording>` then `node build.js`. The MIDI is a sequencer grid, the recording is a band, so the map MIDI-time -> recording-time is a curve with one knot per bar, never a line (Livin': the offset swings from +1.6 s to +2.1 s and back, and the last minute speeds up ~1%). Lessons, each learned the hard way on Livin' on a Prayer:
  - **Drums alone cannot pick the bar.** A groove repeats every beat, so drum matching locks one beat or bar off and trades tempo against offset, and still scores well. Use harmony (MIDI bass+lead chroma vs the recording's chroma, DTW) for the coarse map, then onsets + chroma per bar for the detail.
  - **Search wide enough and never fall back silently.** The first per-hit residual searched +-150 ms, found nothing in the intro and quietly used 0: that was the "misaligned at the start" Carlos heard. A bar with weak evidence should follow its neighbours (Viterbi smoothness), not a default.
  - **Fade-in and fade-out carry no timing.** Chroma on near-silence is noise and DTW wanders there. Mask frames under -45 dB, hold the first/last reliable offset through the fade, and do not pad the MIDI with silence (that forces the DTW end to stretch).
  - **The recording can end before the MIDI.** The real song fades out while the MIDI plays on: `audio.end` (the last audible second) is stored and build.js drops every hit and note after it, so no pad lights in silence.
  - **Measure, do not trust a score.** The report prints the share of drum hits within 40 ms of a recording onset per 30 s, for the straight line and for the bar map. Livin': 43% -> 91%. Check this table after every song and look at any 30 s block under ~80%.
  - **The timeline grid follows the map** (`song.beats`, ms), not a constant tempo, so hits stay on their beat lines.
  - Not handled yet: MIDI with tempo changes or a time signature other than the first (bars are `countIn` beats at the first tempo), and a recording that is a different edit from the MIDI (extra or missing sections would show as a run of low hit-rate blocks). If a new song shows either, extend the tool; do not tune numbers for one song.
